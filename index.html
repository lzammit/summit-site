Summit Trail Support

Summit Trail is a walking game for iPhone. Your real steps and workouts move a hiker up a 2,260 km mountain journey.

How it works

  • Steps from Apple Health move the hiker along the trail.
  • Workouts in Apple Health, like a walking pad, a ride or rowing, move the hiker by the energy they burned, minus the steps you took during them.
  • Active energy is experience. Levels change the hiker's body and gear, and some camps only let strong hikers through.

The hiker isn't moving

  • Check that Summit Trail can read Steps, Active Energy and Workouts: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Summit Trail.
  • Tap the refresh button at the bottom of the trail to read Apple Health again.
  • On a walking pad your phone counts few steps. Record the session as a workout so it counts by its energy (see below).

Walking pads and the Apple Watch

Open Summit Trail on your Apple Watch and tap Start walking pad. The watch records an Indoor Walk, and while it runs your iPhone shows the hiker moving live. If the watch app is missing, open the Watch app on your iPhone and turn on Show App on Apple Watch for Summit Trail. Indoor Walk workouts started from Apple's Workout app count too, once they're saved to Health.

Reminders

The hiker nudges you when you're behind on your step goal, up to 3 times a day and never after you reach it. Change them in the app's Settings, under Reminders.

Your account (optional)

The game works fully without an account. Sign in with Apple in Settings to see your trail on a computer at summittrail.ca and to walk with friends. Apple shares no name and no email with us. In Settings, under Summit Trail account, you can export your data, sign out or delete the account. Deleting it erases your data from the server and ends Sign in with Apple for Summit Trail.

Your data

Without an account, your activity stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is kept with an account.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to support@summittrail.ca. For your data: privacy@summittrail.ca.

Distances, calories and levels are estimates for motivation, not medical advice.
