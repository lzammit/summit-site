Summit Trail

A hiker climbs a 420 km mountain trail, and only your real walking and workouts move him.

How it works

  • Steps from Apple Health move the hiker along the trail.
  • Workouts recorded in Apple Health, like a walking pad, a ride or rowing, move him by the energy they burned.
  • Active energy is experience: levels change the hiker's body and gear, and some camps only let strong hikers through.

The hiker isn't moving

  • Check that Summit Trail can read Steps, Active Energy and Workouts: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Summit Trail.
  • On a walking pad your phone and watch may record few steps. Start an Indoor Walk workout on your Apple Watch so the session counts by its energy.
  • Pull down on the trail to read Apple Health again.

Reminders

The hiker nudges you when you're behind on your step goal or your streak is about to end, up to 3 times a day and never after you reach your goal. Change them in Settings, then Reminders.

Sharing to your own server

Optional. If you run a Summit Trail server at home, open its Connect iPhone page and scan the code with the Camera. If the connection fails, allow Local Network for Summit Trail in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone unless you connect your own server. Read the privacy policy.
